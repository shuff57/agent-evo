# Recommendation letter questionnaire

Fill this out so your teacher can write you the strongest letter possible. Copy this file, answer
each question in a sentence or three, and send the copy back.

## Basic info

- Full name:
- Graduation year:
- Which class(es) did you take with this teacher, and in which years?

## Purpose of this letter

- What is this letter for? (college application and intended major, a specific scholarship, a job or internship, etc.)
- When is the deadline?
- Did the requester specify a length or format requirement? If so, what?

## Qualities to highlight

- What 2-3 qualities or strengths do you most want the letter to emphasize?

## Concrete examples

- Tell one specific story: a challenge you overcame, a project you're proud of, or a time you grew or changed your mind about something. What happened, when did it happen, what did you do, and what was the outcome?

## Academic highlights

- Which specific assignments, projects, or skills from class do you want mentioned?
- How did your grade or your understanding of the subject change over the course, if at all?
- Is there a classroom moment that shows who you are as a student?

## Outside the classroom

- What do you do outside class — leadership roles, clubs, jobs, community involvement, sports, or anything else?

## Goals

- What do you want to study or do next, and why?

## Anything else

- Anything else you want your teacher to know that didn't fit above?
